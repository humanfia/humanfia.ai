# Kaggle — thirteen competitions, audited

<p class="lede">Agents entered real competitions with real deadlines. The results are published
with their provenance, and the two kinds of result are never reported as one.</p>

## The distinction that matters

**Official** means the run was entered before the deadline and holds an exact final position.
The best official finishes are **67 of 3,355** on Student Health Risk and **137 of 6,125** on
ROGII.

**Late** means only the score is real: submitted after the competition closed, scored by
Kaggle, then placed against the frozen board to estimate where it would have landed. Several
sit at the very top — **1 of 1,142** on Google Code Golf 2025, **1 of 670** on WiDS Datathon
2024, **5 of 2,657** on CMI Sensor — and not one of them is a rank, a medal, or evidence of
having competed.

Collapsing the two would make a much better headline. It is also the exact failure mode our
[reviewers exist to catch](/about/#how-we-work).
