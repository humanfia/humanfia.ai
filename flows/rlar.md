# rlar

RLAR, **R**alph **L**oop with **A**gentic **R**eview, has every round of work reviewed and stops
when the reviewer agrees it is done. An actor works in one session that remembers; after each
round a fresh reviewer holds what the actor said to the repository, and its review is the
actor's next prompt. Use it
when "is it done?" should not be answered by the agent that did the work.
