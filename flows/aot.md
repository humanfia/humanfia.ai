# aot

Describe a flow, and get one you can run. A writer drafts it; humanize loads the draft and runs
it on fake agents, one that never says it is done, one that says so at once and one that
answers nothing, and it must end in every case; then a critic reads it fresh. Only a draft that
passes all three lands in this project's `.hmz/flows/`, and any refusal goes back to the writer
word for word for another try.
