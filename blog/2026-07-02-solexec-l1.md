The L1 single-operation track of SOLExec Bench has been confirmed as **first place** at a score
of **0.7608**, on Databricks' entry, with kernels generated by [KDA](/projects/kda). The
previous best was **0.7584**, by Recursive AI.

The margin is 0.0024, which is worth being plain about: this is not a result where an agent
loop found something nobody had thought of. It is a result where a track that many people had
already worked over moved again, by a small amount, because the search was run for longer and
more carefully than a person would sit through.

That is most of what these applications are for. The dramatic wins make better posts; the
narrow ones on picked-over tracks are the better evidence that the method is doing something
repeatable.

The result is currently private on the board and can be made public at any time.
