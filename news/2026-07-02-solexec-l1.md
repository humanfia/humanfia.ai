The L1 single-operation track of [SOL-ExecBench](https://research.nvidia.com/benchmarks/sol-execbench)
has a new leader: the Databricks entry, with kernels generated by [KDA](/projects/kda). The
first-place score was confirmed on 2 July at **0.7608** (Humanfia-reported), and a submission five days later
raised it to **0.7639**, which is where the board has it today.

## A small margin, on a track everyone had tuned

The margin is small, and worth being plain about. Before July, the scores to beat were
Recursive's 0.7584 (9 June) and doubleAI's 0.7587 (29 June).<Sidenote>An earlier version of
this post named Recursive's 0.7584 as the previous best. On the board, doubleAI's 0.7587 is a
few days older than our result and slightly higher.</Sidenote> Today ac4k sits 0.00006 behind.
This is not a result where an agent loop found something nobody had thought of. It is a result
where a track many people had already worked over moved again, by a little, because the search
ran longer and more carefully than a person would sit through.

That is most of what these applications are for. The dramatic wins make better posts; the
narrow ones on picked-over tracks are the better evidence that the method is doing something
repeatable.

## Since then

In September the board moved to a new evaluation stack, v1.1, and rescored the field. On it,
the same entry's best L1 score is 0.7963, third, behind Infinigence AI and SF Tensor; across
all four collections it holds [the most per-kernel first places](/news/2026-06-22-sol-bench-batch).
