In August this flow [took first place](/news/2026-08-18-lean-eval-first) on the
[Lean-Eval](https://lean-lang.org/eval/) leaderboard. That board no longer exists in the same
shape. The maintainers have [frozen a first release](https://lean-lang.org/eval/formalization-evaluation/),
**LeanEval v1**, of **128** problems (published 20 August 2026), and moved the older problems to
an **Archive** of **171**. The leaderboard now shows one scope at a time, and a single "first
place" no longer describes it. Here is where we stand in each.

## What counts

Lean-Eval accepts a solution only when it passes
[Comparator](https://github.com/leanprover/comparator) against the problem's statement. There
are no partial credits and no `sorry`.
